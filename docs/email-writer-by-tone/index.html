Email Writer by Tone

Writes and replies to emails in the tone you choose, professional, polite, friendly or firm, clear and concise.

Add to ClaudeUse in ChatGPT

What it does

Describe what you need or paste an email you received. You get a ready-to-send email with a clear subject line, the right tone for the situation and a specific ask, in as few words as possible. Great for follow-ups, requests, complaints, apologies, declining politely and replying to difficult messages.

Looking for the “Email and Mail Writer” GPT?

Custom GPTs in ChatGPT are being retired. Email Writer by Tone does the same job and works in both Claude and ChatGPT, with clear instructions and no sign-up.

Try asking

  • “Write a professional email asking for a deadline extension”
  • “Reply to this email politely but firmly”
  • “Write a friendly follow-up after a job interview”
  • “Decline this meeting invitation without sounding rude”

Add it to Claude

Claude (web, desktop, mobile)

Open Customize → Directory in Claude, search for Email Writer by Tone and add it. The plugin also works in Cowork and in Claude Code.

Claude Code

/plugin marketplace add SvyatoSllav/claude-plugins
/plugin install email-writer-by-tone@plugins-factory

Use it in ChatGPT

Open Plugins in ChatGPT and search for Email Writer by Tone. Listing in the ChatGPT directory is in review; the package is available in the source repository.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Email Writer by Tone is free and open source under the MIT license. It runs inside your existing Claude or ChatGPT account.

Does it collect my data?

No. It is a set of instructions for the assistant; it does not call external services or store anything. Your conversation stays in Claude or ChatGPT.

Is this the same as the "Email and Mail Writer" GPT?

No, it is an independent assistant built to do the same job after custom GPTs were retired. It works in Claude as well as ChatGPT.

Which languages does it support?

It follows the language you write in. English is the default.
