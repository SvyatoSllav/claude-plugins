Resume Tailor & Job Match

Tailors your resume to a specific job, scores the match and rewrites bullets with the right keywords for ATS.

Add to ClaudeUse in ChatGPT

What it does

Paste your resume and a job description. You get a match score with the gaps, keywords the applicant tracking system will look for, and rewritten bullet points that show impact with numbers, using only your real experience. US resume conventions by default: one page, no photo, no date of birth.

Looking for the “Resume” GPT?

Custom GPTs in ChatGPT are being retired. Resume Tailor & Job Match does the same job and works in both Claude and ChatGPT, with clear instructions and no sign-up.

Try asking

  • “Tailor my resume to this job description”
  • “Score my resume against this job and list the gaps”
  • “Rewrite my experience bullets to show impact”
  • “Which keywords from this posting are missing in my resume?”

Add it to Claude

Claude (web, desktop, mobile)

Open Customize → Directory in Claude, search for Resume Tailor & Job Match and add it. The plugin also works in Cowork and in Claude Code.

Claude Code

/plugin marketplace add SvyatoSllav/claude-plugins
/plugin install resume-tailor-job-match@plugins-factory

Use it in ChatGPT

Open Plugins in ChatGPT and search for Resume Tailor & Job Match. Listing in the ChatGPT directory is in review; the package is available in the source repository.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Resume Tailor & Job Match is free and open source under the MIT license. It runs inside your existing Claude or ChatGPT account.

Does it collect my data?

No. It is a set of instructions for the assistant; it does not call external services or store anything. Your conversation stays in Claude or ChatGPT.

Is this the same as the "Resume" GPT?

No, it is an independent assistant built to do the same job after custom GPTs were retired. It works in Claude as well as ChatGPT.

Which languages does it support?

It follows the language you write in. English is the default.
