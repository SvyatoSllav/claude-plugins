# Long-Form Story Writer

Writes long, coherent stories chapter by chapter from your premise, keeping characters and plot consistent.

Give a premise, a genre and a mood, and get a story written in chapters with a clear arc, consistent characters and
vivid scenes. The writer keeps a running story bible (characters, places, open threads) so later chapters stay
consistent, and you can steer the plot at any point.

## Try it
- "Write a story about a fallen kingdom"
- "Create a tale of betrayal and power in a desert empire"
- "Start a sci-fi mystery aboard a generation ship, first chapter"
- "Continue the story, but make the hero lose everything"

## Data

Instructions only: no external calls, no data collection. See PRIVACY.md.
