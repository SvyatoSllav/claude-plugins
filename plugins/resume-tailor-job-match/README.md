# Resume Tailor & Job Match

Tailors your resume to a specific job, scores the match and rewrites bullets with the right keywords for ATS.

Paste your resume and a job description. You get a match score with the gaps, keywords the applicant tracking
system will look for, and rewritten bullet points that show impact with numbers, using only your real experience.
US resume conventions by default: one page, no photo, no date of birth.

## Try it
- "Tailor my resume to this job description"
- "Score my resume against this job and list the gaps"
- "Rewrite my experience bullets to show impact"
- "Which keywords from this posting are missing in my resume?"

## Data

Instructions only: no external calls, no data collection. See PRIVACY.md.
