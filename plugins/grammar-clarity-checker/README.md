# Grammar & Clarity Checker

Fixes grammar, spelling and punctuation, explains each change and suggests clearer wording.

Paste any text: an essay, an email, a cover letter or a post. You get a corrected version, a short list of what
changed and why, and optional suggestions to make sentences clearer without changing your voice. Works for US and UK
English. Free to use.

## Try it
- "Check my essay for grammar errors"
- "Is this paragraph grammatically correct?"
- "Make this email clearer without changing the tone"
- "Explain the difference between "affect" and "effect" with examples"

## Data

Instructions only: no external calls, no data collection. See PRIVACY.md.
