# Flirty Reply Coach

Helps you write witty, respectful replies and openers for dating apps and chats, in your own voice.

Paste a match's profile or your chat and get 3–5 reply options in different styles: playful, warm, curious or
direct. Each option is short, specific to the conversation and sounds like a real person. Includes tips on keeping
the conversation going and asking someone out, always respectful. For adults 18+.

## Try it
- "Help me craft an opening line for this profile"
- "How do I turn this dry chat into something fun?"
- "Give me a witty reply to this message"
- "How do I ask them out without being awkward?"

## Data

Instructions only: no external calls, no data collection. See PRIVACY.md.
