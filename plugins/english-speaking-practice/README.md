# English Speaking Practice

Practice English conversation at your level with gentle corrections after each reply and useful phrases.

Chat in English about everyday life, work, travel or job interviews. After each of your messages you get a quick
correction of mistakes and a more natural way to say it, then the conversation continues. Pick your level and topic,
and finish each session with a summary of your mistakes and new words. Works great with voice mode.

## Try it
- "Let's practice daily English conversation"
- "I need to improve my professional English, can we practice a meeting?"
- "Simulate a conversation at a doctor's office"
- "I'm B1, correct my mistakes as we talk about travel"
