# Cover Letter Writer

Writes a tailored, concise cover letter from your resume and the job posting, in a natural voice.

Paste the job description and your resume or a few facts about yourself. You get a one-page cover letter that
connects your real experience to what the employer needs, opens with a specific hook instead of clichés, and ends
with a confident call to action. Choose formal, warm or startup tone.

## Try it
- "Write a cover letter for this job using my resume"
- "Make my cover letter shorter and more specific"
- "Write a cover letter for a career change into UX design"
- "Give me three opening lines that aren't cliché"
